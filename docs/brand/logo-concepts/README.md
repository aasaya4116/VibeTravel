# VibeTravel logo concepts

## Selected direction

**02 — Open Passage** is the approved VibeTravel brand mark. Its SVG is the visual source of truth for product, launch, and store assets. Run `node docs/brand/generate-open-passage-assets.mjs` from the project root after intentional source changes to regenerate the native raster assets.

Three symbol-only directions prepared during review; Open Passage is now live in the product assets.

## 01 — Route V

The letterform becomes a tailored route that converges on one precise destination, making the mark direct, ownable, and legible at very small sizes.

## 02 — Open Passage — recommended

An editorial arch and ascending path make discovery feel considered, elevated, and inviting without relying on a plane or globe.

## 03 — Confluence

Three flowing routes meet at one destination, expressing personalized family travel and a shared experience without using a generic travel symbol.

## Design-system grounding

- Near-black `#171512` provides the quiet, premium foundation.
- Warm ivory `#F3EFE7` keeps the mark softer than pure white.
- Portfolio gold `#C8A96E` supplies a luxury focal point.
- VibeTravel copper `#A65636` is reserved for one small signature gesture.
- Every mark uses bold, low-detail geometry and rounded terminals so it survives at a 28–32 px mobile-header size.
